# Handoffs

Requests from one area to another. Write under the heading of the area that owns the fix.
The owner (or the integrator) answers in place: **done** with what changed, or **declined**
with why. Nothing is left unanswered.

Format:

> **From <area>, <date>:** what you need, why, and the exact name, shape or file involved.
> **Answer:** done / declined: ...
